FF Live Draft Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

FF Live Draft ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or account credentials in the app.

Photos and OCR

Draft board photos are used only to extract player names for app functionality. OCR is performed on-device, and we do not store your photos on our servers.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through App Store in-app purchase systems. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and do not track you across apps and websites.

Data Retention

App settings and local app state may be stored on your device to support app features.

Children's Privacy

FF Live Draft is not directed to children under 13.
